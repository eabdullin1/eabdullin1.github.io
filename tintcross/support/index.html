Tintcross Support

Need help? Write to tintcross.support@gmail.com. We usually reply within 3 business days.
To help us find your problem quickly, please include:

  • your support ID (in the game: Settings → Copy support ID);
  • your device model and iOS / Android version;
  • what happened and what you expected.

Frequently asked questions

My progress didn't transfer to my new phone / disappeared after reinstalling.
Tintcross has no accounts and keeps your progress only on your device. It does not sync between devices and cannot be restored after the game is deleted. We're sorry about that.

How do I turn off personalized ads?

  • In the game: Settings → Manage ad consent (Europe, UK, Switzerland) or the Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information switch in Settings (United States). You can change your choice at any time.
  • iPhone/iPad: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, turn off Tintcross.
  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads) to reset or delete your Advertising ID.

You'll still see ads, but they won't be based on your activity in other apps.

Can I remove ads?
No. Tintcross is free, has no in-app purchases and is paid for by ads. Ads appear only between levels after a win, never while you solve a puzzle. Reward videos (for a hint) are always optional.

I watched a video but didn't get the reward.
The reward is given after the video finishes. If the connection drops during the video, the reward may not arrive. Email us your support ID and the time it happened.

How do I delete my data?
Data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the game. To delete analytics data we hold, email your support ID with the subject "Delete my data". Details: Privacy Policy.

The game crashes or freezes.
Update the game to the latest version, restart your device and check there is at least 500 MB of free space. If it keeps happening, email us your support ID, device model and system version. Crash reports reach us automatically, and your support ID helps us find yours.

Can I lose a level?
No. There is no move limit, no timer and no game over: take as long as you like. The number of moves only decides your stars: three stars if you finish within 2 moves of the best solution, two stars within 5.

I find it hard to tell some colors apart.
Every color has its own pattern: stripes for red, dots for yellow, waves for blue. Mixed colors combine the patterns. Make sure Settings → Color patterns is on. High contrast mode makes outlines and colors stronger.

This level seems impossible.
Every level is checked by a solver and always has a solution. Tap a beacon to see which colors it is missing, or use a hint to show where one shape belongs.

Which languages are supported?
English, Russian, Spanish, German, Turkish, French, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Polish, Ukrainian, Indonesian, Japanese, Korean and Chinese (Simplified and Traditional). The game follows your device language; you can change it in Settings → Language.
