Tintcross Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

This policy explains what information is processed when you play Tintcross (the "Game") on iOS or Android, why, who receives it, and the choices you have. The Game has no accounts: we never ask for your name, email or password to play.

1. Who we are

The Game is published by Eduard Abdullin, an individual developer ("we", "us"). For the purposes of the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we are the controller of the data described in section 4, except where a partner acts as an independent controller (section 5).
Contact: tintcross.support@gmail.com.

2. Summary

  • Your progress, stars, hints and settings are stored only on your device. We do not have a server that stores them.
  • We use Google Firebase to understand how the Game is played (analytics), to receive crash reports and to change game settings remotely.
  • The Game shows ads through AppLovin MAX and its partner ad networks. Whether ads are personalized depends on your choices (section 6).
  • The Game has no in-app purchases. We do not process payments.
  • We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
  • You can change your ad privacy choices at any time in the Game's Settings, and contact us at tintcross.support@gmail.com to exercise your rights.

3. Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address (unless you write to us), phone number, contacts, photos, precise (GPS) location, or any content you create. The Game has no chat, no social features and no user accounts.

4. Information processed when you play

4.1. On your device only. Game progress, stars, hints, settings and the state of an unfinished level are saved in the Game's storage on your device. They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the Game deletes them. Progress does not transfer between devices.

4.2. Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase). We collect: game events (for example level started or completed, number of moves, time spent, hints used, settings changed, ads watched), an app-instance identifier generated by Firebase, device model, operating system and version, app version, language, country and approximate region derived from your IP address (Google does not store the IP address itself), and session information. Raw events are also exported to Google BigQuery (Google Cloud, on our behalf) and kept there for 60 days for difficulty analysis. Purpose: to understand which levels are too hard or too easy, to fix problems in the tutorial, and to balance hints and ad frequency. Legal basis: your consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); our legitimate interest in improving the Game elsewhere (Art. 6(1)(f)). Google processes this data on our behalf as a processor.

4.3. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics). When the Game crashes or freezes we receive a technical report: the state of the app at the time of the crash, device model, OS version, app version, free memory and disk space, a Crashlytics installation identifier and the Firebase installation identifier (your support ID, so we can find your report when you write to us). Purpose: to find and fix errors. Legal basis: your consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) — without it no crash reports are sent; our legitimate interest in keeping the Game working elsewhere (Art. 6(1)(f)). Google processes this data on our behalf.

4.4. Remote settings (Firebase Remote Config and A/B Testing). The Game downloads settings such as ad frequency or how many hints you get. To choose the right settings, Firebase uses a Firebase installation identifier, your country, language, time zone, OS and app version. Some players receive different settings to compare variants (A/B tests). Legal basis: your consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) — without it the Game uses its built-in settings; legitimate interest elsewhere (Art. 6(1)(f)).

4.5. Advertising (AppLovin MAX and partner networks). To show ads, the advertising SDKs collect: device identifiers (on Android the Advertising ID; on iOS the IDFA only if you allow tracking, plus the vendor identifier IDFV), IP address, device model, OS, language, network type, app version, approximate location derived from the IP address, and information about the ads you see and interact with. Purpose: to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and, if you agree, personalize ads. Legal basis: your consent for personalized advertising and for storing or accessing information on your device in the EEA, UK and Switzerland (Art. 6(1)(a)); legitimate interest for delivering non-personalized ads, frequency capping and fraud prevention where the law allows (Art. 6(1)(f)).

4.6. Purchases. The Game has no in-app purchases, so we receive no purchase or payment data.

4.7. Notifications. If you allow notifications, the Game schedules them on your device (for example "Today's puzzle is ready"). No data is sent to a server for this.

4.8. When you contact us. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message, and use it only to answer you. Legal basis: legitimate interest in providing support (Art. 6(1)(f)). We delete support correspondence 24 months after the last message.

6. Your advertising choices

  • EEA, UK, Switzerland. On first launch the Game shows a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform, a certified consent management platform under the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework). You can accept, reject or choose specific purposes. Rejecting is as easy as accepting. You can reopen the form at any time in Settings → Manage ad consent. If you reject, you still see ads, but they are not personalized and some networks may not show ads at all. Analytics, crash reports and remote settings stay off unless you consent.
  • iPhone and iPad. The Game asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not shared and ads are not personalized based on tracking. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • United States. Where state law gives you the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising, turn on Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information in the Game's Settings (shown to players in the United States). We also honor this choice if you write to us.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads).
  • Change your mind at any time in the Game's Settings: Manage ad consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland) or Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information (United States).

7. International transfers

Our partners may process data in the United States and other countries outside your own. Where data leaves the EEA, UK or Switzerland, transfers rely on adequacy decisions (including the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the partner is certified) or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to processing, data portability, to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting earlier processing), and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

How to use your rights without an account. Because the Game has no accounts, we cannot link data to your name. To let us find the data from your device:

  1. Open Settings in the Game and tap Copy support ID.
  2. Email it to tintcross.support@gmail.com with your request (for example "please delete my data").
  3. We reply within one month (GDPR) or 45 days (US state laws). We may ask you to confirm the request from the same device.

What we can do: delete analytics data linked to your support ID, delete your Firebase installation identifiers, and answer what we hold. Data stored only on your device you can delete yourself by uninstalling the Game. For data held by ad partners as independent controllers, use the options in section 6 and the links in section 5.

10. Notice for California and other US states

In the last 12 months we collected these categories of personal information: identifiers (device and advertising identifiers, IP address), internet or other electronic network activity (gameplay events, ad interactions), and approximate geolocation (derived from IP address). Sources: your device and your use of the Game. Purposes: those in section 4. We do not collect sensitive personal information.
We do not sell personal information for money. Allowing ad partners to use identifiers for targeted advertising may count as "selling" or "sharing" under California (CCPA/CPRA) and other state laws; you can opt out as described in section 6. We do not knowingly sell or share personal information of consumers under 16. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. You may use an authorized agent to make a request.

11. Children

The Game is rated for all ages but is not directed to children. Its intended audience is 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the higher age set by law in your country). Ads in the Game are limited to content suitable for all ages. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Security

Data sent by the Game travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to our analytics and crash consoles is limited to the people who work on the Game and protected by two-factor authentication.

13. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. For material changes, we will also show a notice in the Game.
